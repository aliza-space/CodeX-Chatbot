# Frequently Asked Questions — CodeX 4.0 and Coders' Club GPREC

**Category:** faq
**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Tags:** FAQ, CodeX 4.0, registration, eligibility, fee, venue, sponsors, speaker, contact

## Event basics

Q: What is CodeX 4.0?
A: CodeX 4.0 is the flagship coding competition organized by Coders' Club of G. Pulla Reddy Engineering College (GPREC), Kurnool.

Q: When is CodeX 4.0?
A: CodeX 4.0 is on 24 September 2026, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM IST, with reporting at 8:30 AM IST.

Q: Where is CodeX 4.0 held?
A: CodeX 4.0 is held at the CSM Computer Labs on the GPREC campus, G. Pulla Reddy Nagar, Nandyal Road, Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh 518007.

Q: How many rounds does CodeX 4.0 have?
A: CodeX 4.0 has exactly two rounds: a Preliminary Round for all registered teams and a Grand Finale for the top-performing teams. The round durations are not published yet.

Q: What is the prize pool of CodeX 4.0?
A: The prize pool of CodeX 4.0 is up to ₹50,000.

Q: Does every participant get something?
A: Yes. Every participant of CodeX 4.0 receives goodies and a hard-copy certificate.

Q: Can CodeX 4.0 lead to an internship?
A: Top-winning teams of CodeX 4.0 have the opportunity to secure internships with WeDevit and other leading tech companies.

## Eligibility and teams

Q: Who can participate in CodeX 4.0?
A: Students of the II, III and IV year from GPREC and other engineering colleges and universities can participate in CodeX 4.0.

Q: What is the team size for CodeX 4.0?
A: A CodeX 4.0 team has exactly 2 or 3 members.

Q: Can a CodeX 4.0 team have final-year students?
A: A CodeX 4.0 team can have at most one final-year (4th-year) student. Two or more final-year students in one team are not allowed.

Q: Can students from different colleges form one team?
A: No. All members of a CodeX 4.0 team must belong to the same college.

Q: Can students from different branches or years team up?
A: Yes. Cross-branch and inter-year teams are allowed and encouraged, as long as the final-year rule is followed.

## Registration and payment

Q: How do I register for CodeX 4.0?
A: Register as a team on https://codex4-0-registration-portal.codersclubgprec.in, or scan the QR code on the official poster.

Q: What is the last date to register for CodeX 4.0?
A: The registration deadline of CodeX 4.0 is 23 September 2026, one day before the event on 24 September 2026.

Q: What is the CodeX 4.0 registration fee?
A: The registration fee is ₹300 per team, not per member.

Q: How can I pay for CodeX 4.0?
A: Payment is processed through Cashfree Payments using UPI, debit card, credit card or net banking.

Q: What is the Team ID?
A: The Team ID is a unique ID, for example CDX4-0001, generated after successful payment verification and also sent by confirmation email to the Team Leader.

Q: I did not receive my confirmation email. What should I do?
A: Wait 15 minutes, check the Spam or Junk folder, and then contact the organizers of CodeX 4.0.

Q: What should I bring on the day of CodeX 4.0?
A: Bring the physical College ID card of every team member, the Team ID or confirmation email, and a hard copy of the CodeX 4.0 Pass.

Q: Is the CodeX 4.0 registration fee refundable?
A: The fee is generally non-refundable. Verified duplicate or excess technical payments are refunded, and a refund is possible if CodeX 4.0 is cancelled without rescheduling.
