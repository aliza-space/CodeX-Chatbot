# CodeX 2.0 — Event Report (24 August 2024)

**Category:** event
**Date:** 2024-08-24
**Tags:** CodeX 2.0, national-level coding competition, winners, past event, Examly, Microcare, ECS Lab

## Overview
CodeX 2.0 was a national-level group coding competition organized by Coders' Club, GPREC, Kurnool, on 24 August 2024. CodeX 2.0 ran from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM at ECS Lab 3, ECS Lab 4 and the Incubation Center of GPREC. The objective of CodeX 2.0 was to give coders a platform to show their skills in a competitive group setting and to encourage teamwork, innovation and real-world problem solving. The target audience of CodeX 2.0 was undergraduate students and coding enthusiasts from across India.

## Participation in CodeX 2.0
- Registered teams: 80. Teams that attended: 80.
- Registered participants: 234.
- Team size: 1 to 3 members.
- Registration fee: ₹300 per team.
- Competition platform: Examly, where registered participants had to register again for the contest.

## Format of CodeX 2.0
CodeX 2.0 had only one round. Round 1 was a coding round with 9 programming problems to be solved in 180 minutes (3 hours). Each problem was worth 10 marks, and the team with the highest total score won. The time taken to complete the problems was the tiebreaker in case of a tie.

## Prizes of CodeX 2.0
- 1st prize (Winner): ₹8,500.
- 2nd prize (1st runner up): ₹6,000.
- 3rd prize (2nd runner up): ₹4,000.
- 4th prize (consolation): ₹2,000.
- 5th prize (consolation): ₹2,000.
- Every participant received a participation certificate.
