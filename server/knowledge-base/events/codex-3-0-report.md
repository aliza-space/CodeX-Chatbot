# CodeX 3.0 — Event Report (25 September 2025)

**Category:** event
**Date:** 2025-09-25
**Tags:** CodeX 3.0, national-level coding competition, winners, two rounds, Smart Interviews, Unstop, Sanjay Samuel, past event

## Overview
CodeX 3.0 was a national-level group coding competition organized by Coders' Club, GPREC, Kurnool, on 25 September 2025. CodeX 3.0 ran from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM at CSM Labs 1, 3 and 4 and the Innovation and Incubation Center of GPREC. The target audience of CodeX 3.0 was undergraduate students and coding enthusiasts from across India.

## Participation in CodeX 3.0
- Registered teams: 94. Teams that attended: 94.
- Registered participants: 282.
- Team size: 2 to 3 members.
- Registration fee: ₹300 per team.
- Competition platforms: Smart Interviews and Unstop, where registered participants also registered for the contest.

## Format of CodeX 3.0
CodeX 3.0 had two rounds.
- Round 1: 8 coding problems in 180 minutes (3 hours). Each problem carried 50 to 100 marks. The top 20 teams with the highest scores qualified for Round 2.
- Guidance session: The teams that did not qualify for Round 2 moved to the auditorium for a guidance interaction with the speaker of the event, Sanjay Samuel, Talent Acquisition Manager.
- Round 2: 3 coding problems in 90 minutes (1.5 hours). Each problem carried 100 marks. The top 3 teams with the highest scores won cash prizes, and the time taken to complete the problems was the tiebreaker.

## Prizes of CodeX 3.0
- 1st prize (Winner): ₹10,000.
- 2nd prize (1st runner up): ₹8,000.
- 3rd prize (2nd runner up): ₹6,000.
- Every participant received a participation certificate.
