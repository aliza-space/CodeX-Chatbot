# CodeX 4.0 — Registration, Payment and Team ID

**Category:** event
**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Tags:** CodeX 4.0, registration, payment, Cashfree, Team ID, CodeX pass, portal, QR code

## Where to register for CodeX 4.0
Registration for CodeX 4.0 is done on the official registration portal at https://codex4-0-registration-portal.codersclubgprec.in. The registration QR code printed on the official CodeX 4.0 poster leads to the same portal. Registration is done as a team and not individually.

## Registration fee for CodeX 4.0
The registration fee for CodeX 4.0 is ₹300 per team. The ₹300 is a flat team fee that covers all 2 or 3 members of the team. Participants do not pay ₹300 separately for each member.

## Registration steps on the CodeX 4.0 portal
1. Log in to the portal first, and then complete the team registration with the participant details.
2. Pay the ₹300 registration fee.
3. After successful payment verification, the portal generates a unique Team ID and shows the Team ID on screen.
4. After successful registration, an option to view the registration details appears on the portal.
5. Download the CodeX 4.0 Pass using the download option on the portal, and bring a hard copy on the event day.

## Payment process for CodeX 4.0
Payments for CodeX 4.0 are processed securely through Cashfree Payments. The accepted payment methods are UPI, debit cards, credit cards and net banking.

## Team ID of CodeX 4.0
A unique Team ID is generated after successful payment verification, in the format CDX4-0001 (example). The Team ID is displayed on screen immediately after verification, and a confirmation email is sent to the Team Leader. Participants keep the Team ID or the confirmation email available for check-in on the event day.

## If the confirmation email does not arrive
If the CodeX 4.0 confirmation email is not received within 15 minutes, the Team Leader should first check the Spam or Junk folder. If the email is still missing, the Team Leader should contact the organizers listed in team/contacts.md.

## What to bring on the event day
Participants must bring the physical College ID card of every team member, the Team ID or registration confirmation email, and a hard copy of the CodeX 4.0 Pass.

## Registration deadline
The registration deadline of CodeX 4.0 is 23rd September 2026.
