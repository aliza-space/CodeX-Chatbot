## Who is Dodagatta Nihar
Dodagatta Nihar is the guest speaker of CodeX 4.0. Dodagatta Nihar is a Tech Educator, Web Developer, ML Engineer, Entrepreneur and 3x TEDx Speaker with 500K+ Instagram followers. The official poster of CodeX 4.0 carries the line "Meet our Guest Speaker: Dodagatta Nihar".

## What Dodagatta Nihar is known for
Dodagatta Nihar is known for making coding and technology accessible through regional-language content, mainly in Telugu. Dodagatta Nihar has conducted workshops and sessions across colleges in India. His public profiles describe content on coding, MERN Stack development, Machine Learning, Data Science and core computer science topics, along with discussions on Artificial Intelligence.

## Platforms and projects of Dodagatta Nihar
- Instagram: @niihaaarrrr, with more than 500K followers.
- YouTube: more than 150,000 subscribers, as stated on his LinkedIn profile.
- Codedale: a platform and software company that Dodagatta Nihar works on, aimed at improving coding education for tier 2 and tier 3 college students through resources in their native language.
- MassCoders: a Telugu coding learning community founded by Dodagatta Nihar.
- Telugu podcasts on technology and coding.

## Why Dodagatta Nihar suits CodeX 4.0
Dodagatta Nihar's work focuses on helping students from smaller cities and colleges build practical, industry-relevant coding skills. His session at CodeX 4.0 gives participants insights into the tech industry, practical learning perspectives and inspiration to build careers in technology and software development.
