# Sponsors and Collaborators of Past Coders' Club Events

**Category:** event
**Date:** 2024-05-04 to 2026-03-25
**Tags:** sponsors, collaborators, Microcare, K Fortune, Dumont Creamery, Codetantra, Smart Interviews, BeyondCampuz, E-Cell IIT Tirupati

## Sponsors of earlier CodeX editions
- CodeX 2.0 (24 August 2024): Microcare Academy was the Diamond Sponsor. The report also records Codetantra, Taj Convention Hall and Frozen Bites as supporters.
- CodeX 3.0 (25 September 2025): K Fortune is recorded as a sponsor, and a Gold Sponsor was felicitated on the day (the name of the Gold Sponsor is not stated in the report).
- CodeX 4.0 (24 September 2026): WeDevit (Technical Sponsor), Havmor, Microcare, Fiarro Pizza and RC Cola. See events/codex-4-0-sponsors.md.

## Sponsors and collaborators of other events
- Code Symposium 2K24 (4 May 2024): sponsored by Dumont Creamery.
- Code Symposium 2K26 (25 March 2026): in collaboration with Smart Interviews, an edtech platform.
- IdeaSprint (21 January 2026): in collaboration with E-Cell, IIT Tirupati, as part of E-Summit '26.
- OUTSYSLAYER Hackathon 2026 (25 February 2026): in collaboration with BeyondCampuz, a startup company.
- Contest platforms used by Coders' Club events: Unstop, Examly, Smart Interviews and HackerRank.

## Repeat supporter
Microcare has supported Coders' Club more than once: Microcare Academy was the Diamond Sponsor of CodeX 2.0 and Microcare is a sponsor of CodeX 4.0.
