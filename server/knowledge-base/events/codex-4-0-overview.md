# CodeX 4.0 — Event Overview

**Category:** event

**Tags:** CodeX 4.0, coding competition, GPREC, flagship event, inter-college, prize pool

## What is CodeX 4.0
CodeX 4.0 is the flagship coding event of Coders' Club, GPREC, Kurnool. CodeX 4.0 is a full-day, inter-college, team-based coding competition designed to test logic, problem-solving and competitive programming skills. The tagline of CodeX 4.0 is "The wait is over!". The organizers' invitation says: "Gather your team. Sharpen your skills. We'll see you at CodeX 4.0."

## Key facts of CodeX 4.0
- Event name: CodeX 4.0 (written on the poster as CodeX 4.O).
- Organizer: Coders' Club, G. Pulla Reddy Engineering College (GPREC), Kurnool.
- Date of CodeX 4.0: 24 September 2026.
- Reporting time for CodeX 4.0: 8:30 AM IST.
- Event timing of CodeX 4.0: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM IST.
- Venue of CodeX 4.0: CSM Computer Labs, GPREC Campus, Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh.
- Team size for CodeX 4.0: 2 to 3 members per team.
- Registration fee for CodeX 4.0: ₹300 per team (flat fee for the whole team).
- Registration deadline for CodeX 4.0: 23 September 2026.
- Number of rounds in CodeX 4.0: two coding rounds.
- Prize pool of CodeX 4.0: up to ₹50,000.
- Guest speaker at CodeX 4.0: Dodagatta Nihar.
- Technical Sponsor of CodeX 4.0: WeDevit.

## Who can participate in CodeX 4.0
Students from GPREC and from other engineering colleges and universities can participate in CodeX 4.0, provided they are in the II, III or IV year of study. Teams need 2 or 3 members from the same college, and each team may include at most one final-year (4th-year) student. CodeX 4.0 suits students who are into competitive programming and also students who simply enjoy solving problems. Full rules are in rules/codex-4-0-eligibility-and-team-rules.md.

## What every participant receives
Every participant at CodeX 4.0 receives goodies and refreshments, and every participant receives a hard-copy certificate. Winners receive prizes from the prize pool of up to ₹50,000, and winner trophies or awards are distributed at the valedictory ceremony.

## Career opportunity at CodeX 4.0
Top-winning teams at CodeX 4.0 have the opportunity to secure internships with WeDevit, the Technical Sponsor, and with other leading tech companies. Internships are an opportunity for top teams and are not guaranteed to every participant.
