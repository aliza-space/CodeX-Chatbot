# OUTSYSLAYER Hackathon 2026 — Event Report (25 February 2026)

**Category:** event
**Date:** 2026-02-25
**Tags:** OUTSYSLAYER, hackathon, BeyondCampuz, AI Model Trainee, Education, Health, winners, Team Soul

## Overview
OUTSYSLAYER Hackathon 2026 was organized by Coders' Club, GPREC, in collaboration with BeyondCampuz, a startup company, on 25 February 2026 from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The venues were CSM Labs 1, 3 and 4 and the Innovation and Incubation Center. The objective of OUTSYSLAYER Hackathon 2026 was to encourage students to build innovative solutions to real-world problems through coding, creativity and teamwork. The hackathon was open to students from various departments.

## Participation in OUTSYSLAYER Hackathon 2026
- Registered teams: 106. Teams that attended: 105.
- Registered participants: 400.
- Team size: 2 to 4 members.
- Registration fee: ₹200 per team.
- Prizes: cash prizes for the winner and the first and second runners-up, and participation certificates for all participants.

## Format of OUTSYSLAYER Hackathon 2026
Participants were divided into three domains based on their departments: AI Model Trainee, Education and Health. Each team worked within its assigned domain, brainstormed ideas, built a solution and submitted the program or project details through a submission link for evaluation.

## Winners of OUTSYSLAYER Hackathon 2026
- Winner: Team Soul.
- 1st runner up: Tech Phantoms.
- 2nd runner up: The Fusion.
