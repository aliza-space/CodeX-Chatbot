# Coders' Club GPREC — Learning Activities and Membership

**Category:** resource
**Date:** ongoing
**Tags:** learning, DSA, classes, contests, interview preparation, recruitment, hackathons, GenAI

## Topics covered in Coders' Club classes
Coders' Club classes cover Math, Data Structures and Algorithms, coding and problem solving. The goal of the classes is to help students write correct, efficient and elegant code and to prepare for coding interviews at product-based companies.

## Contests and practice
Coders' Club conducts coding contests on platforms such as HackerRank. Coders' Club encourages students to clear all rounds of programming contests such as CodeVita, and to participate in hackathons.

## Interview preparation
Coders' Club conducts technical group discussions, coding quizzes and find-the-output exercises on alternate weeks to help students in interview processes.

## Workshops and sessions
Coders' Club has organized Generative AI boot camps (October 2024) and GenAI sessions (January 2026), the Google Gemini Student Ambassador recruitment (September 2025), and the Code Symposium series.

## How students join Coders' Club
Coders' Club has held a recruitment process each year. In 2024, the process consisted of a recruitment exam, interviews and a group discussion. In 2025, Coders' Club held a Wildcard Entry coding round. The recruitment process for future years is announced on the Coders' Club Instagram page @coders_club_gprec.

## Learning roadmaps
Detailed DSA, web development and machine learning roadmaps are not part of this knowledge base yet. For learning guidance, contact Coders' Club at codersclub@gprec.ac.in.
