## Overview
Coders' Club is a technical student club of G. Pulla Reddy Engineering College (GPREC), Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh. Coders' Club is listed on the official GPREC website under Campus Life, Student Clubs. The club exists to help GPREC students build strong problem-solving and coding abilities, because software influences almost every area of the modern world.

## Objectives of Coders' Club
The official objectives of Coders' Club at GPREC are to empower students to do the following:
- Solve problems using Math, Data Structures and Algorithms.
- Write correct, efficient and elegant code.
- Crack coding interviews at product-based companies.
- Compete with students studying at other top institutes.
- Clear all the rounds in programming contests such as CodeVita.
- Participate in hackathons.

## Regular activities of Coders' Club
Coders' Club runs four regular kinds of activities, as described in the official club reports.
- Regular Classes: Regular classes of Coders' Club are guided by Dr. A. Vishnuvardhan Reddy and cover Math, Data Structures and Algorithms, coding and problem solving.
- Review Classes: Review classes of Coders' Club are supervised by club volunteers, who clear the doubts of other students. Review classes also help students interact with their seniors.
- Programming Contests: Coders' Club conducts coding contests on programming platforms such as HackerRank.
- Interview-preparation activities: Coders' Club conducts technical group discussions, coding quizzes and find-the-output activities on alternate weeks to help students prepare for interviews.
