# About G. Pulla Reddy Engineering College (GPREC), Kurnool

**Category:** about
**Date:** ongoing
**Tags:** GPREC, college, Kurnool, address, departments, CSM

## Overview
G. Pulla Reddy Engineering College (GPREC) is an autonomous engineering institution located in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh. GPREC is affiliated to JNTUA, Ananthapuramu, and was established in 1984. GPREC is accredited by NAAC and by NBA. The official college website is https://www.gprec.ac.in.

## Address and contact of GPREC
The address of GPREC is G. Pulla Reddy Nagar, Nandyal Road, Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh, 518007, India. The general office telephone numbers of GPREC are 08518-270957 and 08518-280719. The general email address of GPREC is info@gprec.ac.in.

## Departments at GPREC
GPREC offers programs in Civil Engineering (CIV), Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE), Mechanical Engineering (MEC), Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Computer Science and Technology (CST), Computer Science and Business Systems (CSB), CSE (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) known as CSM, CSE (Data Science) known as CSD, and Humanities and Basic Sciences.

## Student clubs at GPREC
GPREC has many student clubs and forums, including Coders' Club, the T&P Club, ISTE, IEI, NSS and NCC, the Vivekananda Study Circle, Bhuvanavijayam, the CAD and English Club, and IEEE chapters. Coders' Club is the club focused on programming and problem solving.

## Relevance to CodeX 4.0
CodeX 4.0 is hosted on the GPREC campus at the CSM Computer Labs. The CSM department is the CSE (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) department of GPREC.

## Key Campus Facilities & Landmarks
- **Central Library:** Over 66,106 volumes, 14,153 titles, automated with SLIM 21, digital library wing with 12 NPTEL systems, open 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM on working days.
- **Open Air Amphitheatre ("Amphi"):** Sits adjacent to the CSM block quadrangle, tiered stone seating for 800+ people, used for student fests, inductions, and evening hackathon gatherings.
- **Main Cafeteria / Canteen:** Serves South Indian breakfast, lunch thalis, tea/coffee, snacks (8:30 AM – 5:30 PM), supervised by the Canteen Committee.
- **Campus Food Court:** Modern outdoor dining area offering pizzas, burgers, noodles, chaat, fresh juices, shakes, and ice creams (9:00 AM – 6:30 PM).
- **Silver Jubilee Auditorium:** Air-conditioned 1,000+ seat auditorium near the main gate for inaugurations and keynotes.
- **Indoor Stadium & Grounds:** Wooden badminton courts, table tennis, gym, and grounds for cricket, football, basketball.
- **Amenities:** Canara Bank/SBI ATM at main gate, campus health center/dispensary, RO water stations, dedicated visitor parking.
- **Campus Location & Coordinates:** G. Pulla Reddy Nagar, Nandyal Road, Kurnool (15.8073° N, 78.0375° E).
