## Overview
Coders' Club, GPREC has 49 student coordinators in three batches: 17 students of the 2023-2027 batch, 16 students of the 2K25 batch and 16 students of the 2K29 batch. The student coordinators come from the CSE, CSM, CSD, CSB, ECE, EEE, Civil and Mechanical branches of GPREC. The students are guided by the faculty convener Dr. A. Vishnuvardhan Reddy and the faculty coordinators listed in team/contacts.md.

## Branch abbreviations used in this file
- CSE: Computer Science and Engineering.
- CSM: CSE (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning).
- CSD: CSE (Data Science).
- CSB: Computer Science and Business Systems.
- ECE: Electronics and Communication Engineering.
- EEE: Electrical and Electronics Engineering.
- Civil: Civil Engineering. Mech: Mechanical Engineering.
