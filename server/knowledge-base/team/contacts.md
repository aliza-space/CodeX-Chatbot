## Faculty convener of Coders' Club
The faculty convener (also spelled convenor) of Coders' Club, GPREC is Dr. A. Vishnuvardhan Reddy, Associate Professor in the Department of ECS (Computer Science and Engineering) at GPREC, Kurnool. Dr. A. Vishnuvardhan Reddy guides the regular classes of Coders' Club and signs the annual Coders' Club reports.

## Faculty coordinators of Coders' Club
Coders' Club, GPREC has four faculty coordinators who work alongside the convener, Dr. A. Vishnuvardhan Reddy:
- Sri P. Rama Rao, Department of CSE.
- Sri V. Mallesi, Department of CSE.
- Dr. R. Sudheer Babu, Department of ECE.
- Dr. S. Anil Kumar, Department of EEE.
The faculty team of Coders' Club is therefore one convener and four faculty coordinators. Sri P. Rama Rao also presented prizes at Galactic Gamble and IdeaSprint.
