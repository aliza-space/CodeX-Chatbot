# CodeX 4.0 — Eligibility and Team Rules

**Category:** rule
**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Tags:** eligibility, team size, 4th year, same college, roll number, CodeX 4.0

## Who is eligible for CodeX 4.0
Students from GPREC and from other engineering colleges and universities are eligible to participate in CodeX 4.0 if they are in the II, III or IV year of study. First-year students are not listed as eligible for CodeX 4.0.

## Team size rule
Each CodeX 4.0 team must contain exactly 2 or 3 students. A team cannot have fewer than 2 members or more than 3 members.

## Final-year (4th-year) rule
A CodeX 4.0 team may contain zero or at most one 4th-year student. A team with two or more 4th-year students is not eligible for CodeX 4.0.

## Same-college rule
All members of a CodeX 4.0 team must belong to the same college. Students from different colleges cannot be combined into one team.

## Cross-branch and inter-year teams
Students from different branches can form a CodeX 4.0 team. Students from different academic years can form a CodeX 4.0 team, subject to the 4th-year rule. Cross-branch and inter-year teams are encouraged.

## Roll number rule
Each student's roll number can be registered with only one CodeX 4.0 team. Duplicate registration of the same roll number across multiple teams is not permitted.

## Accurate information
All information submitted during CodeX 4.0 registration must be accurate and verifiable. The organizers may verify identity, college enrollment, roll number, academic year, eligibility and team composition.
