# CodeX 4.0 — Refund, Substitution and Disqualification Policy

**Category:** rule
**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Tags:** refund, disqualification, substitution, verification, prizes, certificates, CodeX 4.0

## Refund policy
The ₹300 CodeX 4.0 registration fee is generally non-refundable after successful payment. There is no refund for voluntary withdrawal, absence or disqualification. A verified duplicate or excess payment caused by a technical issue is refundable to the original payment source. A refund is also possible if CodeX 4.0 is completely cancelled without being rescheduled.

## Team-member substitution
A team member substitution may be possible before the registration deadline of 23 September 2026. Participants should contact the organizers with valid credentials to request a substitution.

## Verification
The organizers of CodeX 4.0 may verify participant information, including identity, college enrollment, roll number, academic year, eligibility and team composition.

## Disqualification
A CodeX 4.0 team may be disqualified for any of the following reasons: providing false information, violating the team-size rule, violating the 4th-year rule, registering duplicate roll numbers, plagiarism, indiscipline, or other violations of competition rules. A disqualified team is not entitled to a refund.

## Prizes and certificates
The total prize pool of CodeX 4.0 is up to ₹50,000. All participants receive goodies and hard-copy certificates. Winner trophies and awards, and hard-copy certificates, are distributed physically at the valedictory ceremony. The split of the prize pool across positions is not published in this knowledge base.
