## Geographical Coordinates
- **Institution:** G. Pulla Reddy Engineering College (Autonomous), Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh, India.
- **GPS Coordinates:** `15.8073° N, 78.0375° E` (Latitude: 15.8073, Longitude: 78.0375).
- **Address:** G. Pulla Reddy Nagar, Nandyal Road, Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh - 518007.

## How to Reach GPREC Kurnool (For Inter-College Participants)
- **From Kurnool City Railway Station (KLU):**
  - Distance: ~6.5 km (~15-20 minutes).
  - Transportation: Auto-rickshaws are available 24/7 directly to "Pulla Reddy College Gate" on Nandyal Road. Local APSRTC buses heading toward Nandyal or Mahanandi also stop at GPREC Gate.
- **From Kurnool APSRTC New Bus Stand:**
  - Distance: ~5.0 km (~12-15 minutes).
  - Transportation: City buses and autos are readily available along the highway route.
- **From Hyderabad (RGIA Airport) / Anantapur / Tirupati:**
  - Via National Highway 44 (NH-44) connected directly to Kurnool bypass; follow signs to Nandyal Road / GPREC campus.

## Campus Navigation & Landmark Directory
When you enter through the **Main Entrance Gate (Nandyal Road)**:

1. **CSM Computer Labs (CodeX 4.0 Hackathon Hub):**
   - **Where:** CSM Block, Ground & 1st Floor.
   - **Route from Main Gate:** Enter through Main Gate, walk straight along the main central avenue for 100 meters. Pass the Silver Jubilee Auditorium on your left, continue past the central lawn circle, and the CSM Department Block will be straight ahead.
   - **Distance:** ~180 meters (~2 minutes walk).

2. **Main Cafeteria & College Canteen:**
   - **Where:** South-East zone, adjacent to the sports ground and administrative rear garden.
   - **Route from CSM Labs:** Exit the CSM Block, take the left walkway heading south for 120 meters. The Cafeteria entrance is on your right.
   - **Distance from Main Gate:** ~220 meters (~3 minutes walk).

3. **Campus Food Court:**
   - **Where:** Central Amenities Plaza, next to the Cafeteria and on the pathway leading toward the hostel blocks.
   - **Route from CSM Labs:** Exit CSM Block, walk south for 140 meters toward the shaded outdoor pavilions. The Food Court with juice parlors, chaat, and quick bites is on the left.
   - **Distance from Main Gate:** ~250 meters (~3 minutes walk).

4. **Central Library:**
   - **Where:** Central Academic Quadrangle, right next to the Administrative Block.
   - **Route from Main Gate:** Walk straight for 80 meters; turn right at the main admin corridor. The Central Library entrance with automatic glass doors is right in front of you.
   - **Distance:** ~100 meters (~1.5 minutes walk).

5. **Open Air Amphitheatre ("Amphi"):**
   - **Where:** East of the central lawn, between the CSM Block and the Auditorium.
   - **Route from CSM Labs:** Walk out of the main CSM laboratory exit toward the east courtyard; the semi-circular stone terrace Amphitheatre is immediately visible (30 meters).
   - **Distance from Main Gate:** ~160 meters (~2 minutes walk).

6. **Silver Jubilee Auditorium:**
   - **Where:** West side of the main avenue, immediately visible upon entering the campus gate.
   - **Route from Main Gate:** Just 60 meters from the security checkpoint on your left.

7. **Indoor Stadium & Outdoor Grounds:**
   - **Where:** North-East campus boundary.
   - **Route from CSM Labs:** Walk past the department building northwards toward the sports pavilion (~180 meters).

8. **24/7 ATM & Health Center:**
   - **ATM:** Canara Bank ATM is located immediately beside the Main Security Gate.
   - **Health Center / Dispensary:** Located on the Ground Floor of the Administrative Annex, open during college and event hours.
