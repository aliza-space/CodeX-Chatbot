# Galactic Gamble — Event Report (3 January 2026)

**Category:** event
**Date:** 2026-01-03
**Tags:** Galactic Gamble, team coding competition, HackerRank, bidding round, Code Vaari Paata, II year, III year, winners

## Overview
Galactic Gamble was a team-based, gamified coding competition organized by Coders' Club, GPREC, on 3 January 2026 from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM at CSM Labs 3 and 4 and Lecture Halls 7, 8 and 9. The platform was HackerRank. The objective of Galactic Gamble was to test coding skills, logical thinking and strategic decision-making. Galactic Gamble was open to 2nd-year and 3rd-year students and was organized by the junior members of Coders' Club with support from the seniors.

## Participation in Galactic Gamble
- Registered teams: 72. Teams that attended: 70.
- Registered participants: 144.
- Team size: 2 members.
- Registration fee: ₹50 per team.
- Prizes: cash prizes for the winner (₹750) and runner-up (₹500) of both the 2nd year and the 3rd year, and e-certificates for all participants.

## Format of Galactic Gamble
- Round 1 — Galaxy Spin Challenge: Teams spun a cosmic wheel that decided the planet they landed on, and each planet was linked to specific coding problems. Teams solved 6 problems in 90 minutes to earn points.
- Round 2 — Code Vaari Paata (bidding round): Teams used the points from Round 1 to bid on coding questions, where higher bids carried higher potential rewards.
The report states that the winners were evaluated solely on the scores acquired in the first round.
