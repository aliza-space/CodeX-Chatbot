# IdeaSprint — Event Report (21 January 2026)

**Category:** event
**Date:** 2026-01-21
**Tags:** IdeaSprint, innovation challenge, E-Cell IIT Tirupati, E-Summit 26, national-level, winners, Round 2 IIT Tirupati

## Overview
IdeaSprint was a national-level innovation challenge organized by Coders' Club, GPREC, in collaboration with E-Cell, IIT Tirupati, as part of E-Summit '26. IdeaSprint took place on 21 January 2026 from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM at CSM Lab 1, the Incubation Centre and the CCC Lab. The objective of IdeaSprint was to ignite innovative thinking, creativity and real-world problem solving by challenging teams to design impactful solutions across emerging technology and social domains. IdeaSprint was open to 2nd-year and 3rd-year students. The Coders' Club annual report lists the GPREC round of IdeaSprint as Round 1 of the national challenge.

## Participation in IdeaSprint
- Registered teams: 85. Teams that attended: 85.
- Registered participants: 255.
- Team size: 3 members.
- Registration fee: ₹300 per team.
- Prizes: ₹4,000 for the winner, ₹2,000 for the runner-up, and certificates for all participants.

## Format of IdeaSprint
Teams worked on problem statements from domains such as AI and Machine Learning, Sustainability and Green Technology, Healthcare and BioTech, Smart Cities and IoT, EdTech, FinTech, Cybersecurity, AgriTech, Industry 4.0, Social Impact, and AR/VR. Teams analysed the challenge, brainstormed ideas and proposed feasible implementation approaches within the time limit. Teams were judged on originality of ideas, problem-solving approach, practicality of implementation and clarity of presentation. The top-performing teams were shortlisted for Round 2 at IIT Tirupati, with the chance to win further prizes, internships, mentorship and incubation support.
