## CodeX history

Q: Who won CodeX 3.0?
A: The team !dpsolvers from IIITDM Kurnool (P Sree Charan Reddy and Gopidi Vikranth Reddy) won CodeX 3.0 and received ₹10,000. Unbeatable from GPREC was second and CP Champs from GPREC was third.

Q: Who won CodeX 2.0?
A: The team CP Champs (Vankam Venkata Durga Prasad and Divite Dinesh) won CodeX 2.0 and received ₹8,500. Decoders was second and GPREC1985 was third, with consolation prizes for Bot$$Coders and Mind Benders.

Q: How many teams took part in the earlier CodeX editions?
A: CodeX 2.0 had 80 teams and 234 participants. CodeX 3.0 had 94 teams and 282 participants.

Q: What was the format of CodeX 3.0?
A: CodeX 3.0 had two rounds. Round 1 had 8 problems in 3 hours, and the top 20 teams went to Round 2, which had 3 problems in 90 minutes. Teams that did not qualify attended a guidance session with the speaker Sanjay Samuel, Talent Acquisition Manager.

Q: Was CodeX 2.0 a one-round or two-round event?
A: CodeX 2.0 had only one round, with 9 problems in 3 hours, and it allowed teams of 1 to 3 members.

Q: How much was the prize money in the earlier editions?
A: CodeX 2.0 gave ₹8,500, ₹6,000 and ₹4,000 to the top three teams and ₹2,000 each to the 4th and 5th teams. CodeX 3.0 gave ₹10,000, ₹8,000 and ₹6,000 to the top three teams.

Q: Do teams from other colleges win CodeX?
A: Yes. The winner of CodeX 3.0 was a team from IIITDM Kurnool.

## Other events

Q: What is Code Symposium?
A: Code Symposium is an individual coding competition of Coders' Club with a programming quiz round and coding rounds. Code Symposium 2K24 was on 4 May 2024 (154 registered, ₹50 fee) and Code Symposium 2K26 was on 25 March 2026 (45 registered, ₹50 fee, in collaboration with Smart Interviews).

Q: What was Galactic Gamble?
A: Galactic Gamble was a gamified team coding contest on 3 January 2026 for 2nd-year and 3rd-year students, with a Galaxy Spin round of 6 problems and a bidding round named Code Vaari Paata. It had 72 registered teams and a ₹50 fee per team.

Q: What was IdeaSprint?
A: IdeaSprint was a national-level innovation challenge on 21 January 2026, run with E-Cell IIT Tirupati as part of E-Summit '26. It had 85 teams of 3 members, and the top teams could go to Round 2 at IIT Tirupati. Tech Phantoms won.

Q: What was the OUTSYSLAYER Hackathon?
A: OUTSYSLAYER Hackathon 2026 was held on 25 February 2026 with BeyondCampuz, with 106 registered teams and 400 participants in the domains AI Model Trainee, Education and Health. Team Soul won.

Q: What was Nexus 2K25?
A: Nexus 2K25 on 17 December 2025 had two competitions, WebNex (AI-powered web building) and CodeNex (Data Structures and Algorithms), organized by the GGSA Club with 91 teams of 2 members and a ₹50 fee.

Q: What is the Generative AI Bootcamp?
A: The Generative AI Bootcamp was held by Coders' Club from 16 to 18 October 2024 and introduced generative AI concepts and tools.

Q: Which event platforms does Coders' Club use?
A: Coders' Club events have used Unstop, Examly, Smart Interviews and HackerRank.
