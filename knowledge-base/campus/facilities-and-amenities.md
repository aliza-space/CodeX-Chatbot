# G. Pulla Reddy Engineering College (GPREC) Campus Facilities and Amenities

**Category:** campus
**Date:** ongoing
**Tags:** GPREC, facilities, library, cafeteria, canteen, food court, amphitheatre, auditorium, sports, amenities, campus map

## Overview
G. Pulla Reddy Engineering College (GPREC), Kurnool, spans an expansive, green, 30+ acre campus equipped with state-of-the-art infrastructure for academics, technology hackathons, dining, and extracurricular student life. The official college website is https://www.gprec.ac.in.

---

## Central Library
- **Location:** Central Academic Quadrangle, adjacent to the Administrative Block.
- **Working Hours:** 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM on all working days.
- **Collection & Automation:**
  - Fully automated using SLIM 21 library management software with computerized barcode circulation.
  - Over 66,106 volumes and 14,153 titles covering all branches of Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Basic Sciences, Humanities, Management, and competitive exams (GATE, GRE, CAT).
- **Digital Library & E-Resources:**
  - Dedicated Digital Library wing equipped with 12 high-speed multimedia workstations.
  - Full online access to NPTEL video lectures and course materials across all engineering disciplines.
  - Subscriptions to 84 National and 979 International journals.
  - Access to premier scientific databases including IEEE Xplore, Elsevier (ScienceDirect), ASME, ASCE, INFLIBNET, and DELNET.
- **Facilities:** Spacious, well-ventilated reading rooms with a seating capacity of 150+ users simultaneously, dedicated reference section, reprography (photocopying and printing), and departmental book banks.
- **Borrowing Cards:** 3 cards for 1st–3rd year students, 4 cards for final-year students, and 6 cards for faculty members.

## Campus Canteen & Cafeteria
- **Location:** On-Campus Central Amenities Zone.
- **Operating Hours:** Throughout college working hours.
- **Offerings & Services:**
  - Provides fresh snacks, breakfast, tea, coffee, and wholesome meals for students and staff.
  - Serves as a primary refreshment and social hub for students and faculty between academic sessions.
- **Administration & Oversight:** Overseen by the institutional **Canteen Committee** (comprising faculty members and student representatives) ensuring strict hygiene standards, food quality, and subsidized student-friendly pricing.

## Open Air Amphitheatre ("Amphi")
- **Location:** Central Campus Lawn area, immediately bordering the CSM/CSE academic department block.
- **Description & Purpose:**
  - The Amphitheatre ("Amphi") is GPREC's premier open-air cultural and communal arena.
  - Features tiered semi-circular stone terrace seating accommodating over 800 students.
  - Utilized for Student Induction Programs (SIP), music jams, cultural fests, hackathon social mixers, project exhibitions, club celebrations, and open-air awards ceremonies.
  - Surrounded by manicured lawns and garden trees, providing a refreshing outdoor gathering space.

---

## Silver Jubilee Auditorium
- **Location:** Near the Main Campus Entrance gate, facing the central fountain circle.
- **Capacity & Facilities:**
  - Fully air-conditioned, acoustically engineered indoor auditorium with seating for over 1,000 spectators.
  - Equipped with professional stage lighting, Bose line-array audio systems, dual high-lumen projectors, motorized screens, backstage green rooms, and an executive VIP lounge.
  - Hosts major college ceremonies, university symposia, national conferences, and keynote addresses (including the CodeX 4.0 Guest Keynote by Dodagatta Nihar).

## CSM Department & Computer Laboratories (CSM Block — CSE AI & ML)
- **Official Department:** Department of Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) — CSM.
- **Location:** Dedicated CSM Block situated in the East Academic Quadrangle.
- **Floor-by-Floor Directory:**
  - **Ground Floor:**
    - **CSM Department Office & HOD Chamber:** Administrative headquarters of the CSM Department, faculty cabins, and student counseling desk.
    - **CSM Lab 1 & CSM Lab 2:** Core Programming Laboratories equipped with Lenovo ThinkCentre Neo 50S (i5 12th Gen processors, 16 GB RAM, high-speed SSDs) running Ubuntu/Windows for C, C++, Python, and Java programming.
    - **Drone & Robotics Research Lab:** Specialized research facility for unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), embedded systems, and robotics prototyping.
  - **1st Floor:**
    - **CSM Lab 3, CSM Lab 4 & CSM Lab 5:** Advanced Software & Systems Labs used for Data Structures, Database Management Systems (DBMS), Web Technologies, and Cloud Computing.
    - **Seminar & Tutorial Halls:** Multimedia-equipped halls for guest lectures and workshop sessions.
  - **2nd Floor:**
    - **Intel Unnati AI/ML Lab (CSM Lab 6):** Flagship artificial intelligence and machine learning center established in collaboration with Intel. Equipped with cutting-edge Lenovo ThinkCentre Neo 50S workstations (i5 12th Gen, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB NVMe SSD) dedicated to Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Computer Vision, and Generative AI. This is the **primary arena for CodeX 4.0 Hackathon competition rounds**.
    - **CSM Lab 7 & CSM Lab 8:** Project Development & Incubation Labs used for capstone projects, industry hackathons, and internship incubation.
- **Network & Power Backup:**
  - 1 Gbps redundant campus fiber-optic internet connection with dual-band Wi-Fi coverage across all floors.
  - 100% online uninterrupted power supply (UPS) backed by campus heavy-duty diesel generators ensuring 24/7 non-stop hackathon operation.

## Sports Complex and Indoor Stadium
- **Facilities:**
  - Multipurpose Indoor Stadium housing tournament-grade wooden badminton courts, table tennis arenas, carrom boards, and chess corners.
  - Modern fitness gymnasium with cardio and strength training equipment.

## General Amenities and On-Campus Conveniences
- **Health Centre:** Exclusive on-campus Health Centre operating from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, staffed by a qualified Medical Officer and 3 nurses providing free consultations, first aid, and emergency care.
- **Banking & ATM:** On-campus **Union Bank of India** branch and 24/7 ATM facilities.
- **Campus Book Store:** On-campus store supplying core engineering textbooks, notebooks, and academic stationery.
- **Copier & Telecom Services:** High-speed reprography, photocopying, courier services, and STD/ISD telecom facilities.
- **Drinking Water:** Advanced multi-stage Reverse Osmosis (RO) purified chilled drinking water dispensers installed on all academic wings.
- **Campus Security:** Round-the-clock security personnel, boom barriers at entrance gates, and pervasive CCTV surveillance ensuring a safe campus environment.
- **Visitor Parking:** Dedicated parking zones for two-wheelers and four-wheelers immediately inside the Main Entrance Gate.
